# The tea party

The story where the market meets: an order for a thing on offer is settled at the offer's price, and the
transaction is published. `--story=tea-party`, settings `--tea-party.region` and `--tea-party.ttl`, see the
Play section of the root README. A tea party in a region hears that region's offers and orders only.
